    • About

    Tsoga is a local clothing brand that aims to bring hope and inspiration that all dreams are possible. Venac name for "Wake Up" derived from Northern Sotho. One of the 11 official languages of South Africa.

    Vision Statement

    Tsoga is a sponge like organisation absorbing everyday as it comes. If you can think it, you can achive it. Possibility is real. Local brand exploring the world within the next 40 years.

    Mission Statement

    Tsoga hopes to give hope and inspiration to our customers through our designs. Serving the people with dedication, patience, pride, and unity as a whole from within. Born and breed from the neighbourhoods.
